Seth Seokhee Han

I work on robust computer vision, with a focus on anomaly detection and segmentation under distribution shift.

I’m an undergraduate at Dartmouth working with Prof. Peter Chin, supported by the Presidential Science Scholarship of South Korea. In the summer of 2026, I worked with Prof. Seokhyun Chung at Korea University.

Until April 2028, I’m on leave to serve in the Republic of Korea Army. Replies may be slow, but I read every email and am happy to talk research.
