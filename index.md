I'm a Presidential Science Scholar of South Korea. At <strong class="dartmouth">Dartmouth</strong> I work with Prof. <a href="https://sites.dartmouth.edu/lisplab/sang-peter-chin/">Peter Chin</a>, and at <strong class="korea">Korea University</strong> with Prof. <a href="https://sia.korea.ac.kr/pi">Seokhyun Chung</a>.

My research is on robust visual anomaly detection under distribution shift, plus segmentation and 3D vision. I reframe vision problems around what practitioners need, then solve them with simple methods that drop into existing pipelines.
