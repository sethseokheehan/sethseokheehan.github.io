I work on robust computer vision, with a focus on anomaly detection and segmentation under distribution shift. I reframe vision problems around what practitioners need, then solve them with simple methods that drop into existing pipelines.

I'm a student at <strong class="dartmouth">Dartmouth College</strong>, where I work with <a href="https://sites.dartmouth.edu/lisplab/sang-peter-chin/">Peter Chin</a> and am supported by the Presidential Science Scholarship of South Korea. In the summer of 2026, I worked with <a href="https://sia.korea.ac.kr/pi">Seokhyun Chung</a> at <strong class="korea">Korea University</strong>.
