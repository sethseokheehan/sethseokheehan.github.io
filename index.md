Hi! I'm Seth, a student at Dartmouth studying Engineering Sciences and Music, and a Presidential Science Scholar of South Korea.

My research focuses on robust visual anomaly detection under distribution shift, along with segmentation and 3D vision. I like to reframe vision problems around what practitioners actually need, and to build methods grounded in inductive biases that are simple enough for anyone to drop into an existing pipeline with minimal changes. I work with Prof. <a href="https://sites.dartmouth.edu/lisplab/sang-peter-chin/">Peter Chin</a> at <strong class="dartmouth">Dartmouth</strong> and Prof. <a href="https://sia.korea.ac.kr/pi">Seokhyun Chung</a> at <strong class="korea">Korea University</strong>.
