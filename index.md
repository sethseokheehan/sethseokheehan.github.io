Hi! I'm Seth, a student at Dartmouth studying Engineering Sciences and Music, and a Presidential Science Scholar of South Korea.

My research focuses on robust visual anomaly detection under distribution shift, along with segmentation and 3D vision. I work with Prof. <a href="https://sites.dartmouth.edu/lisplab/sang-peter-chin/">Peter Chin</a> at <strong class="dartmouth">Dartmouth</strong> and Prof. <a href="https://sia.korea.ac.kr/pi">Seokhyun Chung</a> at <strong class="korea">Korea University</strong>. Before college, I co-founded Drice, which built a patented drone system that spots black ice on roads.
